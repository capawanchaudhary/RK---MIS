Production plan: saved meal quantities are exploded through effective menu-to-material recipes and, where used, FG-to-SFG and SFG-to-material BOMs.

Recipe requirement: menu demand ÷ recipe portions × ingredient quantity. Shared SFG requirement: menu demand × SFG quantity per FG.

Actual material: opening stock + purchases − closing stock.

Cost per meal: rolled-up recipe cost plus configured direct expenses for the service group.

Missing recipe links appear as alerts in the plan. Confirm each recipe’s portion basis, ingredient quantity, and unit before using the plan for purchasing or production.
